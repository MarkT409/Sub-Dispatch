This Privacy Policy describes how Lantana Electric LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”), also operating the Crew Dispatch product and SMS program, collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information.

Lantana Electric LLC is the registered business and brand name for this messaging program. Messages may identify the sender as Lantana Electric LLC and/or Crew Dispatch.

SMS / text messaging

If you provide a mobile number to Lantana Electric LLC / Crew Dispatch, or a supervisor adds your number to the crew roster with your consent, we may send transactional SMS related to account sign-in and job assignment alerts.

How you opt in: (1) Web checkbox in Crew Dispatch Settings with consent language naming Crew Dispatch / Lantana Electric LLC; or (2) verbal consent during roster enrollment confirmed by a supervisor. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of employment.

We do not sell mobile numbers or share opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for their marketing.

Information we collect

Name, phone number, email, job/dispatch information, account activity, and device/usage data needed to operate the Services.

How we use information

To operate Crew Dispatch for Lantana Electric LLC, authenticate users, coordinate jobs, send transactional notices, provide support, and protect security.

Sharing

We may share information with service providers who process data for us (including SMS delivery providers such as Twilio), authorized staff, and when required by law. We do not sell personal information.
